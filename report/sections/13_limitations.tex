\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The results of Section~\ref{sec:experiments} hold under conditions that should be stated plainly.

\paragraph{The student body is simulated.} No MIS data was available, so all 1{,}130 students are
generated from confirmed headcounts and deterministic enrolment rules. The cohort sizes, division
structure and batch sizes are realistic, but the individual course choices are not real choices.
Elective demand in particular is modelled as an even split across options, whereas real elective
demand is skewed, and a skewed distribution would place more students in the larger sections and
change which overlaps matter.

\paragraph{Known defects in the generated data.} Two hundred first-year students could not be
placed in \texttt{CS PPS-FY Lec} because the declared section limits do not cover the cohort, and
two M.Tech students overflow the \texttt{PSEC2-EAI} laboratory. Three final-year laboratory batches
contain students from two different lecture divisions as a side effect of the section-filling
order, creating joint enrolments the institution does not intend. The groups
\texttt{BT-Div1}, \texttt{BT-Div2} and \texttt{FY-PP1-A\_} have no members. The offering file still
carries first-year programming laboratory sections 16 to 41, which are no longer wanted; removing
them requires re-importing file 12 after uncommitting any saved solution, because an incremental
import still deletes unmatched classes within the offerings it mentions.

\paragraph{Scope is one department and one term.} Everything reported here concerns the Department
of Computer Engineering for Spring 2026. A university-wide instance would be several times larger
and would introduce inter-departmental room contention that this dataset does not exercise.

\paragraph{The residual conflicts are unresolved.} The 40 first-year conflicts were diagnosed and
localised to four laboratory placements against the reserved lecture block, but not fixed. Two
remedies were identified---re-timing those laboratories, or adding explicit different-time
distribution constraints between the laboratory subpart and the reserved block---and neither has
been implemented or measured.

\paragraph{Soft-constraint quality is poor.} The room preference hit rate is 12.59\,\%, and 17 of
291 assignments land in the unavailable-laboratory pool. The solver is satisfying the hard
constraints and spending the soft budget elsewhere. No tuning of the room-preference weight was
attempted.

\paragraph{The evaluator has a broken check.} The instructor identity check scores 0\,\% because it
compares differently formatted names, so that column of the preference score carries no
information and drags the aggregate down. The aggregate preference score of 6.29 should therefore
not be quoted on its own.

\paragraph{The learning layer is untested on real data.} Arms L and R have never been run on a COEP
instance; the only bake-off that ran on real data covered the three baselines and used an instance
that was already conflict-free. There is no held-out term, and training used a single instance, so
no statement about generalisation is possible. The performance figures quoted for the layer come
from a 120-class synthetic instance.

\paragraph{One experimental artefact is inconsistent.} In the weight sweep, the recorded summary
and one of the fifteen stored per-seed metric files disagree for weight 5, apparently because that
single run was repeated after the summary was written. Both readings are reported in
Section~\ref{sec:experiments} and neither changes the conclusion, but the run was not repeated to
settle the matter.

\paragraph{Dataset provenance is recorded incorrectly in the files.} Four of the generated
documents carry a hard-coded attribute naming snapshot 240, while the dataset was in fact generated
from snapshot 242. The provenance had to be reconstructed by counting entities. The generators
should write the snapshot they were actually given.

\paragraph{No instructor-facing interface exists.} Instructor availability and preferences are read
from the Taasika snapshot. The interface proposed in Semester~VI, through which staff would enter
their own constraints, has not been built, so the preference data is only as current as the
snapshot.
